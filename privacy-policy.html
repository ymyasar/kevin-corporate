Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Who We Are

This website is operated by Kevin Nguyen Headshots, a corporate and executive photography studio based in Austin, Texas. We provide professional headshot sessions for individuals, executives, and corporate teams across Austin and Central Texas.

Questions about this policy may be directed to KevinHeadshots@gmail.com.

What Data We Collect

We collect only the information you voluntarily submit through our contact and booking form. This includes:

Name, company name, email address, phone number, session type preference, and any additional message you choose to include.

No payment data is collected through this website. Any payment processing for sessions is handled separately and directly with Kevin Nguyen Headshots outside of this site.

How We Use It

The information you submit is used solely to respond to your booking inquiry and schedule your photography session. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties for marketing or other purposes.

Cookies & Analytics

This website does not currently use cookies or third-party analytics tracking. If analytics are added in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Third-Party Services

This site uses Google Fonts to load typography. Google Fonts may log your IP address when fonts are requested. You can review Google's privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy.

Data Retention

Booking inquiry information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and fulfil any resulting session. We do not store form submission data beyond that purpose.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To make such a request, please contact us at KevinHeadshots@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time frame.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects when the most recent changes were made. Continued use of this website after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
