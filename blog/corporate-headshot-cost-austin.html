How Much Do Corporate Headshots Cost in Austin?

Kevin Nguyen July 28, 2026 8 min read

Corporate headshots in Austin generally run $290 to $1,500 for an individual and $1,800 to $2,500 for a team session, depending on how much time you need, how many finished images you walk away with, and whether the photographer comes to your office.

At Kevin Nguyen Headshots the numbers are fixed and published. A standard headshot session is $350. An executive session is $425. A team session starts at $1,800. There is no quote process, no per image surcharge at the end, and no negotiating over what “finished” means.

That is the short answer. The rest of this page explains what actually drives that number, because the sticker price is not where most people get caught out.

What you are actually paying for

A headshot fee is not a fee for pressing a shutter. It covers five things, and photographers weight them differently.

Session time. Thirty minutes gets you one look. Seventy five minutes gets you three, with room to change clothes and reset. Time is the single biggest cost driver for individuals.

Direction. Most people have never been photographed professionally and do not know what to do with their hands, their jaw, or their shoulders. A photographer who can direct you into a natural expression in the first five minutes is the difference between a headshot you use and one you quietly replace.

Retouching. This is where pricing models diverge sharply, and it is covered in its own section below.

File delivery. You should receive high resolution files plus web and LinkedIn crops, licensed for business use. If a photographer delivers only low resolution proofs and charges to unlock the full files, that is a second invoice waiting to happen.

Turnaround. Same day and 48 hour delivery cost more to operate than a two week queue. Kevin Nguyen Headshots guarantees 48 hours on every session, and same day on the LinkedIn Refresh.

Individual, executive, and team sessions

Three different jobs, three different price points.

LinkedIn Refresh — $290. Thirty minutes, one look, five selects, delivered the same day. This is the right session if you already know what you want and you need it live on your profile before an interview or a launch.

Standard Headshot — $350. Forty five minutes, two outfit changes, two fully retouched images. The default choice for most professionals updating a profile that is three years out of date.

Enterprise Headshot — $425. Seventy five minutes, up to three outfit changes, three fully retouched images. Built for people who need more than one usable look, such as a formal shot for the firm directory, a softer one for LinkedIn, and something with more personality for a speaker bio.

Personal Branding — $1,500. Two hours, ten retouched images, plus environmental and product shots. This is a content session rather than a headshot session. Founders, coaches, and anyone who needs a quarter of website and social imagery in one sitting book this one.

Corporate Team — from $1,800. The entry tier covers up to ten people. The four hour tier at $2,500 covers up to seventy five. Both are on site, and both are flat rate.

The per image trap

Here is the part that decides whether a team shoot costs what you were quoted.

There are two retouching models in this market.

Flat rate. The session fee includes finished, retouched images for everyone photographed. The number you agree to at booking is the number on the invoice.

Per image. The session fee covers the shooting day. Every retouched image is billed separately, commonly between $100 and $150 each. The quote looks lower. The invoice does not.

Work it through on a twenty person team.

Under a per image model, a session fee in the low four figures plus twenty finished images at $125 each adds $2,500 in retouching alone on top of the shooting fee. One image per person, which is the bare minimum, and you are past $3,500 before anyone asks for a second option.

Under flat rate, twenty people fall inside the $2,500 four hour tier. Everyone photographed gets a finished image. That is the invoice. If someone wants an additional retouched frame later, it is $50, not $125.

Neither model is dishonest. But one has a floor and the other has a ceiling, and you should know which one you are signing before the shoot rather than after. The question to ask any photographer you are considering is short: is the retouching included, and for how many images per person?

On site versus studio

For an individual, a studio session is usually fine. You are one person, you can travel, and a controlled space gives the photographer full lighting control.

For a team, on site wins almost every time, and the reason is arithmetic rather than aesthetics.

Sending twenty employees to a studio means twenty round trips, twenty scheduling conflicts, and a no show rate that guarantees you are booking a second session for stragglers. A photographer who brings a full lighting setup to your office turns that into a single morning. People step away from their desk for eight minutes and go back to work.

On site also fixes consistency. Everyone is shot under the same light, on the same background, on the same day. A directory where half the team was photographed in a studio in March and half at a conference room window in September looks exactly like what it is.

Kevin Nguyen Headshots runs on site setups across the Austin metro and can photograph between 200 and 400 individuals in a single day when the schedule calls for it.

What moves the price up

Five things, in rough order of impact.

Headcount. The single biggest factor for teams, and the reason tiers exist at all.

Locations. One office is one setup. Three offices across the metro is three setups and three travel windows.

Turnaround. A guaranteed 48 hour delivery costs more to staff than a two week queue. Rush requests below that compress it further.

Outfit changes and looks. Each additional look costs session time, which is why the $350 and $425 tiers differ mainly in minutes.

Extra retouched images. At Kevin Nguyen Headshots these are $50 each. Under a per image model they are the main event.

What does not move the price: asking for the files in a usable resolution, asking for a LinkedIn crop, or asking for commercial usage rights. Those should be standard.

Austin specifics

Austin pricing sits slightly above the national median for a mid sized market, largely because the tech and legal sectors here refresh their team imagery on a real cycle rather than once a decade.

A few local notes worth knowing.

Downtown and the central business district. Parking and load in are the constraint, not the photography. Sessions here are easiest scheduled around a conference room with a free wall.

Round Rock, Cedar Park, Georgetown, and Pflugerville. All inside the standard service area. Suburban offices tend to have better on site space than downtown towers, which usually makes the shooting day faster.

Law firms. Firm wide directory shoots follow the same team pricing, starting at $1,800 for up to ten attorneys and scaling to seventy five plus. There is more detail on the attorney headshots page.

Hybrid teams. If half your staff is remote, book the on site day around your densest in office day rather than trying to bring everyone in. Stragglers can be caught in a studio session afterward.
