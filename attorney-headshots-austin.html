Common Questions About Attorney Headshots in Austin

How long does each attorney spend in front of the camera?

Approximately five minutes. Attorneys walk in, receive brief on-set direction, work through a few natural expressions and postures, and are done. Most find it faster than expected. The session is designed to fit between client calls and court appearances, not to take attorneys away from their day.

How much space do you need at our office?

A small conference room works. We need roughly 12 feet of depth and 8 feet of width to set up lights and backdrop. We don't need a large open floor plan, a reception area, or a dedicated studio. If you have a conference room that seats six people, we can work there.

Can you photograph our whole firm in one day?

Yes. We photograph up to 75 attorneys in a single full day. A mid-size firm of 15–20 attorneys is typically done in a half-day morning session. Kevin works with your office manager before the shoot to build a rotating schedule — attorneys receive a time slot and show up for five minutes, so no one is waiting around.

What happens when we hire a new attorney mid-year?

We keep your firm's lighting setup, backdrop selection, and crop settings on file after the original session. When a new attorney joins, they're photographed to match the existing roster exactly — same look, same feel. Your team page stays visually consistent no matter when someone comes on board.

Are files delivered ready for the State Bar of Texas, Avvo, Martindale-Hubbell, and Super Lawyers profiles?

Yes. Every attorney receives their photos in square (1:1) and portrait (4:5) crops, in both web-optimized and print-resolution versions. Those files are ready to upload directly to your firm website bio, State Bar of Texas profile, Avvo, Martindale-Hubbell, Super Lawyers, and LinkedIn — without anyone resizing or reformatting anything. Your office manager never opens a photo editor.

What should our attorneys wear?

Solid colors in business-professional attire photograph best — navy, charcoal, white, and muted tones read cleanly against any backdrop. Avoid busy patterns or large logos. We recommend attorneys bring two or three options to the session; Kevin will advise on what reads best on camera for your firm's backdrop color.

What does it cost — are there per-image fees?

Pricing is flat-rate: individual attorney sessions start at $350, and firm team sessions start at $1,800 for up to 10 attorneys and scale to 75+. There are no per-image fees, no charges for extra selects, and no additional invoices for file formats or retouching. What's quoted is everything.

How fast do we get the files?

Fully retouched files are delivered to a private download gallery within 48 hours of your session — guaranteed. Every attorney on your team receives their complete file set at the same time. You'll never wait more than two business days to update your website, directories, or firm profiles.
