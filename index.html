Common Questions

How much do corporate headshots cost in Austin?

Individual sessions start at $350 and include multiple looks, on-set direction, and retouched final files delivered within 48 hours. Corporate team packages start at $1,800 for up to 10 people and scale to full-day shoots of 75+. All pricing is flat-rate — no hidden fees for extra selects or file formats. For a full breakdown of what drives the price, see the corporate headshot cost guide.

Can you photograph our whole team at our office?

Yes. On-site shoots are one of our most-booked formats. We bring a complete portable studio setup — lights, backdrops, and equipment — directly to your Austin office or conference room. We can turn around a team of 20 in a single half-day with no disruption to your workday.

How fast do we get the photos?

Edited, retouched files are delivered via a private download gallery within 48 hours of your session — guaranteed. Rush same-day delivery is available on request for an additional fee. You’ll never wait a week to update your team directory.

What should I wear for a corporate headshot?

Solid colors and business-professional attire photograph best — navy, charcoal, white, and muted tones hold up well against any background. Avoid busy patterns, large logos, or very light grey (it can wash out). Bring two or three options to the session and we’ll choose together based on what reads best on camera.

Do you offer headshots for LinkedIn profiles?

Absolutely. Our LinkedIn Refresh session ($290) is a focused 30-minute shoot designed specifically for profile photos — one polished look, five retouched selects, delivered the same day. It’s the fastest way to upgrade your professional presence without booking a full session.
